Questions

Does Matscape show the steps?

Yes. Tap Steps under any result to see how it was reached, one operation at a time. For many operations you can choose the method, for example cofactor expansion or row reduction for a determinant.

Are the answers exact?

Yes. Matscape keeps fractions, square roots and multiples of π exact where it can, and switches to decimals when you prefer.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Everything is calculated on your device and the app works offline. Backup and sync to your own Google Drive is optional.

Can I copy results into LaTeX?

Yes. Results and whole step-by-step solutions copy as LaTeX, ready to paste into a paper or notes.

Does it work on tablets and foldables?

Yes. Matscape has a layout for phones held upright or sideways, tablets with the inspector beside your work, and book-style and flip foldables, including tabletop posture.

Is Matscape on iPhone and iPad?

Matscape is coming to the App Store. It is available on Google Play now.
