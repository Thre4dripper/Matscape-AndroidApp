Every matrix tool.
One elegant workspace.

Matscape is a matrix calculator that works like a page of notes. Keep your matrices at the top, write expressions underneath, and every answer updates as you type, set like a textbook.

Get it on Google Play App Store: coming soon
  • Every step, worked outTap Steps under any result to see how it was reached, one operation at a time, with a choice of method: cofactors or row reduction, Gauss–Jordan or the adjugate, elimination or Cramer’s rule.
  • Exact answersFractions, square roots and multiples of π instead of long decimals, and decimals whenever you prefer.
  • Every operationDeterminant, inverse, rank, RREF, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, LU, QR, Cholesky and SVD, matrix exponentials and more.
  • Phones, tablets and foldablesA layout for each screen, from a phone on its side to a tablet with the inspector beside your work.
  • Your data stays yoursNo account, no ads, no tracking. Optional backup goes to your own Google Drive.
  • Copy as LaTeXResults and whole solutions paste straight into a paper or notes.
