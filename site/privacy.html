Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Applies to Matscape on Android and iOS

In short: Matscape has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Your workspaces stay on your device. If you choose to connect Google Drive, they are also kept in your own Drive, in a private folder only Matscape can read. We, the developer, never receive any of your data.

What Matscape stores

The matrices and expressions you write, the history of expressions you have entered, and your settings. They are saved on your device, in the app’s private storage.

Your phone or tablet may include that storage in its own backups, such as Android’s backup to your Google account or an iCloud backup on iOS. Those backups are run by Google or Apple under your account and their terms.

Backup and sync with Google Drive (optional)

Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings → Backup & sync. If you do:

  • You sign in with Google and allow one permission, drive.appdata. It lets Matscape create and read files only in its own hidden folder in your Google Drive. Matscape cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Each of your devices saves one file there with your workspaces, history and settings, and reads the files from your other devices to keep them in step.
  • The files go straight between your device and Google, encrypted in transit. They do not pass through or get stored on any server of ours.
  • Matscape shows the email address of the connected Google account in Settings. It is kept only on your device.
  • The files count toward your Google storage. You can see and delete them at any time: on drive.google.com, open Settings → Manage apps, find Matscape and choose “Delete hidden app data”.
  • You can stop syncing at any time in Settings → Backup & sync → Disconnect, which also withdraws Matscape’s access to your Drive. You can also remove the access from your Google Account’s security settings.

Google’s handling of your Google Account and Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Matscape’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Matscape uses that information only to back up and sync your own data for you, never for advertising, and never shares it with anyone.

What Matscape does not do

  • It does not collect analytics or crash reports.
  • It does not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Deleting your data

Delete workspaces inside the app, or uninstall Matscape to remove everything on that device. To remove the Drive copy, see “Delete hidden app data” above.

Children

Matscape is suitable for students of any age and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
