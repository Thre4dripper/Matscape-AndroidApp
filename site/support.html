Support

Help & tips inside the app (in the menu) covers how everything works. Here are the questions people ask most.

How do I keep my work if I change phones?

Open Settings → Backup & sync and connect Google Drive. On your new phone, install Matscape, connect the same Google account, and your workspaces come back.

How do I use Matscape on a phone and a tablet?

Connect the same Google account on both. They sync when you open the app, a few seconds after you stop editing, and when you tap Sync now. If you change the same workspace on both before they sync, both versions are kept so nothing is lost.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your device, and in your own Google Drive if you connected it. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and how to delete it.

An answer looks wrong

Tap Steps under it to see how it was worked out. If a step is wrong, please tell us, with the matrices and the expression.

Get in touch

Report a problem or suggest a feature by opening an issue on GitHub. Please include your device and what you expected to happen.
