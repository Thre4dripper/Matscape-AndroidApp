Matscape on every screen

One adaptive app. The layout follows the space it has and how the device is folded. Every frame below is the live prototype, so you can tap around in any of them.

  • CompactPhones and closed foldables. Single column with the keypad at the bottom.
  • MediumSmall tablets in portrait. Sidebar with navigation and matrices; workspace beside it.
  • ExpandedLarge tablets in landscape. Sidebar, workspace and a permanent inspector.
  • BookFoldables opened flat. Two panes split at the fold; nothing sits on the crease.
  • TabletopHalf-folded on a desk. Results above the fold, keypad below.
  • GlanceSmall cover screens. Read-only view of the latest results.

Each layout also has its own page in the layouts folder, starting with iPhone · compact. Sizes for iPhone Duo and the Android foldables are approximate, in points / dp. On real devices the fold position comes from the system; here you switch it with the buttons above each foldable. The phone-only prototypes are still available in an iPhone frame and an Android frame.
